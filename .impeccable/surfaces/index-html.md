Mode: Operate. Mobile-first installable web app; every screen shares one shell (bottom tab bar, top bar, content column).

Audience and job: adults with ADHD and autistic adults, often on a phone, often stuck or overwhelmed. Job: get one small thing started, calm down, or see today's next step, within seconds.

Constraints: PRODUCT.md commitments (free, no ads, no account, local data, never punishing, not medical). Keep every existing function. ## Direction contract

THESIS: Your garden is a city lot of numbered raised beds, one bed per kind of activity, and each finished activity plants something in its bed. Refuses the category's soft rounded wellness cards, gradient hero and glowing dark glass.

OWN-WORLD: Asphalt-dark ground, painted timber bed frames, chalk-white text, kale green growth, safety orange reserved for the single next action. A sunny-concrete light variant mirrors it. Stencil display face only for plot numbers, labels and big numbers; a hyperlegible sans for everything read. Small 3–4px corners, dashed chain-link borders for zones, 1px hairlines; no glows, no gradients, no glass.

STORY: The visitor sees their lot at today's real sky above a city skyline, understands that every bed is something they did, and is offered exactly one next thing in orange. Everything else is a quiet tap away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: date, Quiet and Music buttons. Skyline band with the moving sun or moon and stars over rooftops. Below it the lot: a 3×3 grid of numbered timber beds, plants inside each bed matching its activity, the suggested bed outlined orange. Under the lot, a stencilled plot sign: the next action's name and one orange button. Then three quiet links (Breathe, Check in, Today). Bottom tab bar.
